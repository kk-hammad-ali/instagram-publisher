so it carries the keywords. Instagram now indexes caption *text* for search, not just
hashtags — so the words in the body do more work for discovery than the tag block does.

post to post — pasting an identical block on every upload reads as automation. Nothing
here makes a post go viral; the hook and the first two seconds do that. Tags just decide
who gets shown it.

## Two honest notes

**Hashtags don't make things go viral.** They haven't for a couple of years. Reach on
Reels comes from watch-through and shares. The tags decide *which* non-followers get
shown it; the first two seconds decide whether it travels. If you want more from these
posts, the highest-leverage change is a stronger opening frame, not a bigger tag block.

**Keep the captions on your side of the line.** Everything here talks about the shoot,
not what the supplement does. That's deliberate — the moment a caption on a public
account claims a health outcome, you're carrying the brand's regulatory risk for them.

---

## Posting plan

Your account is at **53 posts**. Adding 24 grows it by nearly half, so how you release
them matters as much as the captions.

**Always post in threes.** Each row is a single artwork split across three tiles. Post one
or two of a row and the grid reads broken until you finish it. Post the **right tile
first, then middle, then left** — Instagram fills newest-first from the top left, so
reversing the order is what makes the row assemble correctly.

**Suggested cadence:** one row every 3–4 days, all three posts within the same hour. That's
eight drops over about a month. Slower than dumping all 24, but the grid is never
half-built, and you get eight separate chances at reach instead of one.

**Order to release them in.** Lead with the rows that show range rather than the ones that
show volume:

1. **H** — Albus & Inspire. Fashion and retail. Proves you're not only a supplements shop.
2. **F** — Rewell & Wonder. Beauty, the highest-budget category on this list.
3. **C** — Nurture Pharma. Pharma is the hardest sell and the best credential.
4. **A** — Natural Herbal Hub. Your strongest single frame is A1.
5. **D** — TruFibr. Food and nutrition.
6. **E** — Weight loss. Includes the faceless UGC piece, which is a service worth naming.
7. **B** — Herbal supplements.
8. **G** — Personal care. Last, because it's blocked on the Lushly watermark anyway.

**At 3.7k followers**, most of your reach on these will come from non-followers through
Reels. That traffic is decided by watch-through, not by the caption — so the caption's job
here is conversion, not discovery. Someone lands on the reel, likes the look, reads the
caption, and DMs. Every one above is written for that moment.
